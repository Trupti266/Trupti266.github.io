Project 02
Claims Management Transformation
Challenge

Claims were handled as email cases rather than a governed process.

Claims arrived by email
Batch and serial information was often missing
QA review was not consistently tracked
Credit notes could move without structured root-cause control
Redesign

Build traceability, ownership and financial control into the workflow.

Structured intake with defect information
Claim case linked to the underlying order in ERP
48-hour QA and 8D review
Credit note only after QA sign-off
Traceable defect history including supplier recovery
Process decision: money moves only after a documented quality decision, with the claim linked back to the source transaction.

ERP, Master Data & Governance

Use the system for rules that should not depend on memory.

Across both projects, I used ERP controls, data standards, documentation and ownership rules to make processes more consistent and easier to govern.

Order completeness

Defined mandatory commercial and technical information before release. IT supported the technical configuration of mandatory-field controls.

Customer & article data

Audited Kundenstamm and Artikelstamm, corrected discrepancies and standardized required information and naming conventions.

Pricing logic

Maintained recurring Sonderkonditionen directly in ERP master data so pricing did not depend on salesperson memory.

Workflow

Defined the business logic for automated notifications and worked with IT to implement them.

Governance

Introduced SLAs, SOPs, Arbeitsanweisungen, templates and clear exception ownership.

Monitoring

Used ERP reporting to make order status, delays, invoice backlog and process blockers visible.

ERP environment: hands-on cimERP by Aptean. The underlying process concepts are transferable to comparable order, master-data, pricing and billing processes in SAP S/4HANA.

Process Management Approach

A repeatable way of working.

Across O2C, claims and commercial operations, I use the same core approach: understand the current process, fix the root cause, standardize the operating model and then manage exceptions with data.

How I approach process management

Diagnose where the process actually breaks.
Move controls to the point where defects can be prevented.
Make ownership, rules and documentation explicit.
Use systems and data to sustain the improvement.
