Project 01
End-to-End Order-to-Cash Transformation
Business Problem

Incomplete orders created downstream delays and blocked billing.

Orders entered with incomplete commercial or technical data
Manual handoffs between Sales, Purchasing and Production
Missing information identified after order release
Repeated clarification loops and unclear ownership
Delivery delays and growing open-invoice backlog
Process Redesign

Introduced order completeness controls, automated handoffs and SLAs.

Standardized required customer, commercial and technical information
Order release only after defined completeness requirements
ERP-supported controls and automated notifications
Clear cross-functional ownership and response SLAs
Standardized handoff into fulfilment and billing
Key improvement: moved validation to order entry and made completeness a prerequisite for release.

Project 02
Claims Management Transformation
Business Problem

Unstructured claim handling caused delays, weak traceability and uncontrolled credit-note processing.

Claims handled mainly through email
Incomplete batch and serial information
QA review not consistently tracked
Limited linkage between claim, source order and root cause
Process Redesign

Standardized intake, QA approval and ERP-linked traceability.

Structured claim intake with required defect information
Claim linked to the original order in ERP
48-hour QA and 8D review
Credit note only after QA approval
Traceable defect history and supplier recovery
Key improvement: linked claims to the source transaction and introduced a defined QA approval before financial action.
