ERP, Master Data & Governance

Use the system for rules that should not depend on memory.

The redesign reduced manual coordination by moving repeatable business rules into cimERP and making data quality part of the process.

Order completeness

Defined mandatory commercial and technical information before release. IT supported the technical configuration of mandatory-field controls.

Customer & article data

Audited Kundenstamm and Artikelstamm, corrected discrepancies and standardized required information and naming conventions.

Pricing logic

Maintained recurring Sonderkonditionen directly in ERP master data so pricing did not depend on salesperson memory.

Workflow

Defined the business logic for automated notifications and worked with IT to implement them.

Governance

Introduced cross-functional SLAs, standard templates, SOPs and clear exception ownership.

Monitoring

Used ERP reporting to make order status, delays, invoice backlog and process blockers visible.

ERP environment: hands-on cimERP by Aptean. The underlying process concepts are transferable to comparable order, master-data, pricing and billing processes in SAP S/4HANA.
